Learnix Research Help & Support

Welcome to Learnix Research Portal. Here is how to create an account and access the Devil's Advocate RAG Engine:

Step 1: Enter your full name and registered email address, then click Next.

Step 2: Check your email inbox for a 6-digit verification OTP code. Enter the code and click Verify.

Step 3: Create a strong password containing at least 8 characters, upper/lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols.

If you encounter any issues receiving your email OTP code, check your Spam/Junk folder or contact support at learnix.research@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Learnix Research values your academic privacy and data integrity.

1. Information We Collect

We collect your full name, email address, and hashed passwords solely for authenticating your session on the Peer-Review RAG Engine.

2. Data Security & Encryption

All passwords are salted and hashed using PBKDF2 HMAC SHA-256 standard encryption. Plain text passwords are never stored or logged.

3. No Third-Party Selling

Your research papers, query telemetry, and user information are never sold to external data brokers.
